## 1. Concrete Solution Description

**What it is:** A pre-processing AI agent layer for HR recruiting and onboarding, deployed as a lightweight add-on to existing HRIS/ATS setups (primarily Personio, secondarily Softgarden and Greenhouse), purpose-built for German mid-size companies (200–2,000 employees). It is not an ATS replacement. It is not a standalone SaaS platform that requires data migration. It is an intelligent processing layer that sits upstream of the HRIS — ingesting applications from wherever they arrive (email, career page upload, job board forwarding), doing the analytical work, and pushing structured, human-ready outputs back into the HRIS and into the recruiter's inbox.

**Product type:** Agent layer / workflow automation product. Two separable modules deployable independently or together: (1) **NeoRecruit** — a pre-ATS CV intake, scoring, and shortlisting agent, and (2) **NeoDesk** — an HR self-service chatbot and onboarding workflow orchestrator. Both modules share a common compliance layer (audit logging, human-override enforcement, Works-Council-ready documentation kit).

**Target user:** HR Manager or Recruiter at a German Mittelstand company, 200–1,000 employees, running Personio or a comparable HRIS. A 1–3 person HR team that is simultaneously the buyer and the daily user. They are already digitalized enough to use Personio, but not so large that they have dedicated HR IT staff or budget for enterprise tooling.

**Deployment context:** The company has a Personio or Softgarden account. The company may or may not have a works council. Applications arrive via email (still common in German mid-market), via job portal (Indeed, StepStone, XING), and via career page uploads. NeoEmployee deploys NeoRecruit as an email-monitored or webhook-triggered intake pipeline — not dependent on the Personio CV file API, which is documented as broken. Candidate data never leaves EU-hosted infrastructure.

### Core Capabilities

**1. Multi-channel CV intake and parsing (not Personio-dependent)**
Applications arriving by email (monitored inbox), PDF upload portal, and job board API forwarding are captured by the agent before ATS ingestion. CV text is extracted using a licensed parser (Affinda for German-language CV support, 95% accuracy, EU data residency configurable). This sidesteps the Personio API gap entirely and is the architectural foundation that makes everything else possible.

**2. Ranked shortlist with plain-language candidate summaries**
For each open role, the agent produces a ranked list of the top N candidates (configurable, default top 10) with a structured one-paragraph rationale per candidate: which criteria are met, which are absent, what the recruiter should look for in a first call. The ranking is based on semantic matching against the job description plus a configurable scoring rubric (required skills, experience level, location). The ranker uses Claude for reasoning, not keyword matching. The output is a PDF or structured dashboard view sent to the recruiter — not a filtered-out pile that no human ever sees. All candidates below the shortlist are logged, not deleted, with their position in the ranking visible. This design is required by GDPR Art. 22 and EU AI Act human-in-the-loop obligations.

**3. Draft candidate communications in German**
After shortlist review, the recruiter marks candidates as "advance," "hold," or "decline." The agent drafts the appropriate communication in German: an interview invitation with scheduling link, a hold acknowledgment, or a DSGVO-compliant rejection letter that does not expose the AI's reasoning (which would create AGG liability). The recruiter edits and sends; the agent does not send autonomously.

**4. AI-generated job ad drafting against market language benchmarks**
When a new role opens, the recruiter provides role title, department, and 3–5 bullet points on requirements. The agent drafts a complete job ad in German, formatted for the target job boards (StepStone, Indeed, XING), using language patterns from effective ads in the same role category. Outputs include a gender-neutral formulation check (AGG compliance) and a note flagging any language likely to reduce application diversity.

**5. HR self-service chatbot grounded in company documents (NeoDesk)**
An employee-facing chatbot embedded in the company intranet or as a Slack/Teams bot, with a RAG knowledge base built from uploaded HR documents: employee handbook, leave policy, parental leave process, expense rules, benefit summaries. The bot answers employee questions in German with citations to the source document. For anything outside its knowledge base (individual payroll queries, specific legal situations), it routes to the HR manager with a drafted response suggestion. The chatbot connects to Personio's API for live absence balance lookups.

### User Journey Sketch

**Day 1 (Setup — roughly 2–4 hours):**
The HR manager shares Personio API credentials and an email address dedicated to application intake. NeoEmployee's onboarding engineer connects the intake email monitor, configures the unified ATS API layer (Merge.dev or direct Personio connection), and uploads the company's three to five most common HR documents to the chatbot's knowledge base. The HR manager fills in a role scoring template for the first active job posting — essentially a checklist of must-have and nice-to-have criteria. No data migration. No new login portal for employees yet.

**First open role (within the first week):**
A new batch of 80 applications arrives over three days via email and the career page. The agent processes each one as it arrives: parses the CV, scores it against the open role's criteria, adds it to the ranked shortlist. On day four, the HR manager receives an email: "Your shortlist for [Role Name] is ready — 10 candidates ranked, with summaries." She opens the PDF or web dashboard. She reads 10 one-paragraph summaries instead of scanning 80 CVs. She marks 4 candidates as "advance," 3 as "hold," 23 as "decline" in the dashboard. The agent drafts German-language emails for all categories. She reviews and sends them with two clicks. The full ranked list of all 80 candidates, with their individual scores and the scoring rubric used, is stored in the audit log — available for any subsequent Betriebsrat inquiry or candidate right-of-access request under GDPR.

**Minutes 1–10 in NeoDesk:**
The HR manager uploads the employee handbook (PDF), the leave policy document, and the expense reimbursement rules. The chatbot is immediately queryable. She types "What's the notice period for parental leave?" and confirms the answer is correct. She shares the chatbot link with three employees to test. Within ten minutes, the chatbot answers three routine questions she would have otherwise answered herself by email.

## 2. Value Proposition

**Core value (one sentence):** NeoEmployee cuts recruiter screening time per role from 5–15 hours to under 90 minutes by delivering a ranked, explained shortlist from every application channel — built from day one to pass German Works Council review, GDPR Art. 22 compliance, and EU AI Act Annex III requirements without additional consulting work.

**Differentiation from Personio/Softgarden/Greenhouse AI add-ons:**
The incumbent tools fail in three specific ways that NeoEmployee solves:

- Personio's CV parser fails on 2 of 3 CVs in independent testing, and its Recruiting API v2 does not return CV files. NeoEmployee does not depend on Personio's parser. It processes CVs from any intake channel using a German-qualified parser (Affinda), producing reliable extraction before data ever enters Personio.
- Greenhouse, Softgarden, and Personio's AI features are designed for US hiring norms and English-language CVs. NeoEmployee is built to understand German qualification structures: Ausbildung, Meister, Fachwirt, Fachhochschule vs. Universität distinctions, and the standard German Lebenslauf format with Anschreiben and Zeugnis attachments. A candidate with a Meister qualification is not misclassified as under-qualified because the system does not recognize the certification.
- No global vendor has built GDPR Art. 22-compliant, EU AI Act Annex III-documented, BetrVG-compatible AI recruiting tooling as a default. US vendors are retroactively patching compliance. NeoEmployee ships the Works-Council-ready documentation kit, the human-override mechanism, the audit log, and the bias testing report as standard product features — not consulting engagements on top of the product fee.

**Retention hook:** The audit log and ranked history accumulate over time. After six months of use, NeoEmployee holds a documented, searchable record of every candidate who applied, how they were scored, what rationale was used, and what decision was made — exactly the documentation required under EU AI Act Art. 12 and for any Betriebsrat audit. Losing the product means losing the compliance documentation trail. No German mid-market company, once it has passed its first Betriebsrat review using NeoEmployee's documentation kit, wants to start that process over with a new tool.

## 3. Feature Set

### MVP
The MVP must deliver core value for the primary pain — CV screening overload — with enough compliance infrastructure to survive a German Works Council review. It must be buildable in under 6 months by a 14-person bootstrapped team using the existing stack.

- **Multi-channel CV intake pipeline:** Monitor a dedicated email inbox (IMAP/Gmail API) and a configurable upload webhook; capture PDFs and DOCX files; pass to parser. No Personio API dependency required.
- **German-language CV parsing via Affinda:** Extract structured fields (personal data, education with German qualification type classification, work history, languages, skills) from German-format CVs including Lebenslauf + Anschreiben combinations. 95% accuracy, EU-hosted, GDPR DPA available.
- **Role-specific scoring rubric builder:** HR manager defines 5–10 criteria for a role (e.g., "minimum 3 years in logistics," "Ausbildung in Spedition oder Logistik," "B2 German minimum") with weights. Stored as a structured template per job posting. Can be duplicated for recurring roles.
- **Semantic candidate ranking with Claude:** Each parsed CV is scored against the rubric using Claude with a structured prompt that outputs a score per criterion, a confidence note, and a plain-German paragraph explaining the match. Output is deterministic for the same input (temperature 0, logged prompt version).
- **Shortlist PDF/dashboard output:** Top N candidates (configurable) delivered by email to the recruiter as a formatted PDF with candidate summaries. A web dashboard version with mark-advance/hold/decline controls. The full ranked list of all candidates (not just shortlist) accessible in the dashboard.
- **Draft candidate communications in German:** On recruiter marking decision, agent drafts German-language email per decision type (advance / hold / decline). Recruiter edits and sends; agent does not send autonomously.
- **Tamper-resistant audit log:** Every scoring event logged with timestamp, job posting ID, candidate ID (hashed), scoring rubric version, raw score, and LLM response. Stored in EU-hosted database. Exportable as PDF for Betriebsrat review or GDPR Art. 15 candidate access requests.
- **Works-Council-ready documentation kit:** Pre-filled Betriebsvereinbarung template, EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation template, DPIA template for the recruiting use case — all populated with NeoEmployee product specifics. HR manager submits this to the Betriebsrat before go-live. NeoEmployee provides one onboarding session to walk through it.
- **NeoDesk RAG chatbot (MVP version):** Knowledge base loader accepting PDF/DOCX documents. RAG pipeline over company HR documents using Claude. German-language Q&A with source citation. Slack bot integration or embeddable web widget. Live absence balance lookup via Personio API where available.
- **Personio structured data push:** After shortlist review, push candidate status updates (advance/decline/hold) to Personio candidate records via Personio API v2 (which does support status updates even without CV file access). Reduces double-entry.

What is explicitly out of scope for MVP: interview scheduling automation, people analytics dashboards, job ad generation (deferred to V2), onboarding workflow orchestration (deferred to V2), mobile app, multi-HRIS support beyond Personio.

### V2 — Defensibility Layer

V2 is about stickiness and breadth. Each V2 feature deepens data lock-in, expands the use case surface, or raises the switching cost.

- **Job ad generation with market language benchmarking:** Draft job ads in German from role template, benchmarked against language patterns from high-performing ads in the same category (using a periodically updated reference set). AGG gender-neutral language checker built in. Diversity language scoring flag. Delivers measurable time savings for a second frequent HR task.
- **Structured onboarding workflow orchestrator (N8n-based):** Configurable onboarding checklist that automatically triggers tasks: IT access request email (templated), document upload requests to the new hire, welcome message from line manager (drafted by agent), day-1 schedule send. Timeline configurable (preboarding start, Day 0, Week 1, Week 4). Integrates with Personio hire date and role data. Solves the 64%-of-employees-have-no-preboarding problem with a concrete workflow product.
- **Candidate pipeline analytics per role:** Conversion funnel per job posting (applied → shortlisted → advanced → hired), time-to-shortlist, average score distribution, top rejection reasons by criterion. Exportable. Allows HR managers to see which criteria are filtering most aggressively and adjust rubrics. This is the first step toward people analytics without requiring a separate analytics product.
- **Multi-HRIS support via Merge.dev:** Add Softgarden, Greenhouse, HiBob, and d.vinci as first-class ATS integrations using a unified API middleware layer rather than per-client custom connectors. Enables growth beyond the Personio-only segment without rebuilding integration logic per customer.
- **Bias monitoring report (quarterly):** Automated analysis of scoring patterns across candidate demographics (to the extent CVs contain inferable attributes). Flags criteria or scoring patterns showing statistically anomalous outcomes. Delivered as a PDF report to HR manager and formatted for Betriebsrat review. This is both an EU AI Act compliance feature and a trust-builder that distinguishes NeoEmployee from black-box tools that caused the "AI doom loop."
- **HR chatbot escalation routing with drafted responses:** When NeoDesk routes a question it cannot answer, it surfaces a pre-drafted German response for the HR manager to review and send — reducing the HR manager's work even for the cases the bot cannot fully handle. Escalation pattern database builds over time and feeds back into knowledge base refinement.
- **NeoDesk integration with IT provisioning (lightweight):** Email-triggered IT onboarding request to the IT department with role-specific access checklist — bridging the documented gap where IT onboarding is always outside the HR tool scope.

### Long-term vision (3 years, if V1 and V2 succeed)

If NeoEmployee has 80–150 paying mid-market customers by 2029, the accumulated data asset — anonymized, aggregated patterns of which hiring criteria predict successful hires across German mid-market industries — becomes the foundation for a qualitatively different product:

- **Skills intelligence layer:** Matching internal candidates to open roles, surfacing internal mobility options, and providing skill-gap visibility per department. Emerging from the same scoring rubric data already being collected.
- **Predictive analytics for German Mittelstand benchmarking:** Time-to-hire by role type, offer acceptance rate benchmarks, compensation range signals — aggregated and anonymized across the customer base. A "Mittelstand HR Benchmark" report distributed to customers as a retention and upsell driver. This is the Personio-killer moat: Personio has the HRIS data, but NeoEmployee has the recruiting signal and the quality-of-hire tracking.
- **Compliance automation product:** Generated Works Council agreements, EU AI Act risk assessments, DPIA documentation, and AGG audit reports as a standalone product line sold to German HR teams regardless of whether they use NeoRecruit or NeoDesk. NeoEmployee's operational experience with BetrVG negotiations, accumulated across 80+ client deployments, is encoded into templates and generation logic that no US vendor can replicate. This becomes a legal-tech adjacent offering in the largest labor-law-dense market in the EU.
- **HRIS-agnostic agent platform:** If Personio declines further or loses market share, NeoEmployee has built integrations for Softgarden, Greenhouse, HiBob, and d.vinci — meaning it is not a Personio add-on but a DACH-native HR intelligence layer that works with whatever HRIS the customer uses.

## Directional Warnings

Three findings from the research that should constrain product and messaging decisions and not be rationalized away:

**Warning 1 — Do not pitch "automated filtering." Pitch "human-speed shortlisting."**
GDPR Art. 22 as interpreted by the ECJ C-634/21 ruling makes fully automated pre-screening that plays a "decisive role" a legally grey practice in Germany. The product must be designed and sold as a tool that accelerates human judgment, not replaces it. The recruiter sees and acts on every shortlisted candidate. Every below-shortlist candidate is logged and viewable. The AI ranks; the human decides. This is not a legal caveat to add to marketing copy — it is a product architecture requirement. Any messaging that implies the system "filters out" candidates without human review will trigger Works Council objections, AGG liability exposure, and procurement rejections.

**Warning 2 — The AI recruiting "doom loop" means buyers are skeptical by default.**
SHRM's own headline is "Recruitment Is Broken. Automation and Algorithms Can't Fix It." A regional bank shut down a $2M AI recruiting investment in 2025. 19% of companies knowingly use AI screening tools they know ignore qualified candidates. NeoEmployee enters this market with tailwind on the problem side and significant headwind on the trust side. The product must demonstrate explainability — not as a compliance checkbox, but as the primary UX design principle. Every ranking must be accompanied by a plain-German explanation that a recruiter can read, understand, disagree with, and override. Sales conversations should lead with the explainability and human-override design before mentioning AI efficiency.

**Warning 3 — AI-generated CV inflation degrades the long-term signal value of resume screening.**
40–80% of applicants now use AI to write or optimize their CVs. This means the information density in CVs is declining: everything looks like a match for everything. NeoEmployee's scoring rubric approach partially addresses this (weighting verifiable hard credentials like Ausbildung type and years of experience over soft skill language) but does not solve it. The long-term defensibility of a CV-screening-centric product is uncertain. V2 should introduce skills verification signals that go beyond CV text — for example, integrating structured work sample or skills test prompts into the candidate intake workflow, or weighting Zeugnisse and reference document analysis more heavily than CV self-description. The roadmap should explicitly include a "beyond CV text" capability as a hedge against the continued degradation of CV-as-signal.
